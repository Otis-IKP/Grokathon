Title: Yale NEPO warm intros

A built in application in web and imessages application.

- Uses grok as someone you can text when you want to get added to a club

Say you want to become part of YSIG but you don’t know anyone on the YSIG board. 

You can add friends in the app.   
Text Grok I want to be on YSIG.   
It will link you to one of your friends who knows someone in YSIG, either by being part of the club or also having them added as a friend.   
Search through your contacts, and can craft the message to send to your friend or you paste it in the chat and it will send.
